# .impeccable.md — Name Vitals Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

People who typed their own name into a search bar — probably out of casual curiosity but open to being surprised. They arrive light-hearted and leave with a mix of amusement and mild existential weight ("am I really one of the last Mortimers?"). They're not analysts — they want to feel something, not read a dashboard. The discovery moment matters more than the data volume.

### Brand Personality

**Curious. Dramatic. Warm.**

Like a nature documentary about names — David Attenborough narrating the slow disappearance of "Mildred." Playful on the surface, but the extinction framing carries real weight. The interface should feel like finding an old name in a census record: intimate, a little uncanny, and genuinely interesting.

### Emotional Goals

- **Delight** at the unexpected (a rising name nobody expected, a beloved name in freefall)
- **Mild drama** around extinction and decline — names are cultural artifacts, their loss is worth pausing on
- **Warmth and personal connection** — this is about _your_ name, not abstract data

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Evolve the editorial warmth but add drama**: The warm serif foundation is right, but the current design is too quiet. Bolder type hierarchy, more confident use of negative space, more expressive status treatments.
- **Light default + dark mode via `prefers-color-scheme`**: The dark mode should feel archival midnight — navy/ink blacks rather than pure dark, warm off-whites rather than stark whites.
- **The medical/vital-signs metaphor is an asset**: "Vitals" evokes both medical records and vital statistics (census/genealogy records). Typography and layout can nod to historical documents without being literal.
- **Anti-references**: Avoid dashboard-software aesthetic (Notion, Linear, Vercel). Avoid the sterile census-form look. Not a data product — a story product.
- **Reference feel**: Longform editorial + natural history museum exhibit. Numbers that feel weighted, not just informative.

### Design Principles

1. **Drama earns its weight** — use tension (contrast, scale, color) selectively, especially around extinction and decline. Don't apply drama to everything or it loses meaning.
2. **Warmth over efficiency** — this is a personal experience, not a productivity tool. Let breathing room and typography do the heavy lifting rather than information density.
3. **Status is the hero** — the verdict (Rising / Declining / Extinct) is the emotional payoff. Typography, color, and layout should build toward it.
4. **Light playfulness, heavy moments** — the experience is curious and fun in its baseline state, but endangered and extinct results should feel appropriately sober.
5. **Every element earns its place** — no decorative complexity for its own sake. Simplify aggressively except where drama is intentional.
